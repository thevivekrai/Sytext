Questions, answered.

What is Sytext?
Sytext is an AI-first unified inbox. It puts WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Slack, SMS and more into one place, tells you who needs a reply by AI priority, and drafts the response for you.
Do I need an account to try it?
No. The demo inbox has 15 sample conversations across five networks, and you can try it without creating an account.
Does it send messages automatically?
Never. Sytext drafts replies, but you always review and press send yourself.
Which networks can I connect?
WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram DMs, Guffshuff, Matrix (through bridges), Slack and SMS. You can also use a Telegram bot.
Is my chat history stored on a server?
Chats live on your devices. The server only keeps the links and the latest 40 messages per chat (plus pinned or starred), so a small free database is enough.
What AI models can I use?
The built-in demo AI works by default. Signed in, you can use the free shared AI (OpenRouter or Gemini), or bring your own key for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, Grok, or any OpenAI-compatible service.
How do priority tags work?
Each allowed chat is marked Urgent, Normal or Low, and tagged by topic: Question, Follow-up, Deadline, Payment, Order, Meeting, Work, Personal, Promo or FYI. Marking a chat done clears the tags that asked something of you.
