Questions, answered.

Is Sytext a voice typing app?
No, Sytext is an AI-first unified inbox. It puts WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Slack, and SMS into one interface, tells you who needs a reply, and drafts the response for you.
Do I really never touch the keyboard?
You still press send. Sytext generates the draft, but nothing is ever sent automatically. You always have the final review.
Is it always listening to me?
No. Sytext doesn't listen to your microphone. It reads your incoming messages on the networks you've linked, and only when you've allowed AI access to those specific chats.
Which apps does it work with?
WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram DMs, Slack, SMS, Matrix, and your own chat site (Guffshuff).
How accurate is the transcription?
Sytext doesn't transcribe audio—it reads text. For the text it drafts, AI results are checked against strict schemas before they ever reach your screen.
Do I need an internet connection?
Yes, to sync with the networks. But Sytext keeps a local copy of your chats on your device, so you can read history offline.
What happens after I download?
Sytext is a web app. There's nothing to download. Just sign in at sytext.onrender.com, connect a network, and your inbox is ready.
